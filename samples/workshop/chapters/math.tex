\section{Mathematics}\label{sec:math}

Subscripts outside math mode cause a hard error --- comment out the next line to get a
warning-only build:

The expression a_2 + b^2 should be wrapped as $a_2 + b^2$.

When the error is fixed, this aligned environment is fine:
\begin{align}
  E &= mc^2 \\
  F &= ma
\end{align}
